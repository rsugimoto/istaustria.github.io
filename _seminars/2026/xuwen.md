abstract: |
  Thin shells are a central component of physical simulation, appearing widely in geometric design, digital garments, soft robotics, and other deformable physical systems. They remain particularly challenging to simulate due to large deformation, rich collision and contact, and frequent involvement in design and interaction tasks. In this talk, Xuwen Chen will present her work on thin shell simulation, including parametric representations, continuous collision detection, multiphysics coupling, and design based on differentiable simulation. Together, these works explore how to build accurate and efficient contact-aware thin-shell simulation frameworks, and how differentiable simulation can further support learnable physical environments for design and control.
